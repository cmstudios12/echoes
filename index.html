Our Story

Born from a passion to share hope through gospel music.

Echoes of Mercy was formed in 2022 in Tonga, Mpumalanga. Founded by Sipho Michael Ngomane, the group was created from a desire to spread a message of hope and love through music and make a positive impact on listeners.

The group’s sound blends traditional gospel, contemporary Christian and urban gospel influences while keeping the message of Christ at the centre.

FaithMusic that points listeners to Christ and encourages spiritual growth.
HopeSongs created to uplift spirits and bring encouragement.
Positive ImpactA commitment to touching lives positively through music.
ConnectionCreating space for gospel music lovers to celebrate faith together.
Purpose

More than performance, a ministry through music.

Echoes of Mercy exists to use music as a channel for the Gospel, encouragement, worship and connection.

Mission

To spread the Gospel of Jesus Christ through music, inspire hope, bring joy to listeners, touch lives positively, encourage spiritual growth and create a platform for gospel music enthusiasts to connect and celebrate their faith.

Vision

To become a leading gospel music group recognized globally for exceptional music, commitment to spreading the Gospel and positive impact on the lives of listeners.
